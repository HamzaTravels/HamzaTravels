About Hamza Travels

Hamza Travels is a trusted travel agency specializing in complete booking and documentation solutions for passengers and expatriates in Saudi Arabia and India. Our mainstream focus centers on providing international and domestic Air Ticket Bookings, Hotel Reservations, Dummy Tickets, Indian Passport Renewal, New Passport Applications, and VFS Appointment support.

For Indian nationals residing in the Kingdom, we extend end-to-end file preparation for passport renewals, scheduling online submission slots, and managing flight returns efficiently. Additionally, we facilitate legal Umrah Visa support for residents wishing to bring their parents, spouses, and relatives directly from India to share peaceful family stays.

At Hamza Travels, customer comfort, honesty, and professional delivery define our values. Whether planning a routine holiday visit, looking for budget hotel stays, or handling crucial passport applications, we make travel effortless.
