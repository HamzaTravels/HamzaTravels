Frequently Asked Questions

Realistic answers regarding travel, Umrah, and consular assistance.

How does flight ticket booking work with Hamza Travels?

Share your preferred dates and route with us. We check schedules across available airlines, share competitive fare options, and issue confirmed tickets subject to airline availability upon payment.

What Umrah services do you offer?

We assist eligible pilgrims with Umrah visa guidance, flight ticketing arriving in Jeddah or Madinah, hotel accommodation in Makkah and Madinah, and local transfer arrangements, subject to current Saudi regulatory guidelines.

Can Hamza Travels renew my Indian passport directly?

No travel agency can issue passports. We provide professional application and documentation assistance — helping you complete online government forms, verify photo and document checklists, and schedule appointments at authorized VFS centers.

Do you guarantee VFS appointment slots?

No agency can guarantee appointment slots because they are controlled directly by VFS Global and relevant authorities. We assist by checking appointment availability, understanding application requirements and preparing document checklists.

What are "Flight Reservations for Visa Documentation"?

Genuine flight reservation and itinerary options may be provided for travel documentation and visa applications, subject to airline availability and the specific requirements of the relevant embassy, consulate or authority.

How are Makkah and Madinah hotels arranged?

We provide selected lodging options ranging from budget-friendly hotels with scheduled shuttle services to accommodations closer to the Holy Harams, giving you clear distances so you can decide comfortably.
