Frequently Asked Questions

Realistic answers regarding flight bookings, passport assistance, and visa documentation.

How does flight ticket booking work with Hamza Travels?

Share your preferred travel dates, departure, destination, and passenger details. We check live schedules across available airlines, share competitive fare options, and issue confirmed tickets upon payment.

Can Hamza Travels renew my Indian passport directly?

No travel agency can issue passports. We provide professional application and documentation assistance — helping you complete online government forms, verify photo and document checklists, and schedule appointments at authorized VFS centers in Saudi Arabia.

Do you guarantee VFS appointment slots?

No agency can guarantee appointment slots because they are managed directly by VFS Global and diplomatic authorities. We assist by monitoring appointment availability, checking document readiness, and preparing checklists.

What are "Flight Reservations for Visa Documentation"?

These are genuine flight reservation itineraries provided for embassy visa applications, subject to airline availability and the specific requirements of the relevant consulate or visa processing authority.

What assistance do you provide for Saudi Work Visa Medical & Stamping?

We provide guidance on generating Gamca/Wafid medical slips, coordinating document readiness, police clearance guidance, and navigating Saudi work visa stamping requirements.
